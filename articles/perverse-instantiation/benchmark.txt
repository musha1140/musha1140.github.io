## Curve justification, as the record supports it

The hardware problems solved in-engine are real and documented: ring oscillation in the register cell,
a write window racing the feedback lap, a self-latching riser, a dead tunnel segment hidden by that
latch, a dust end powering a lever block, branch signal death, and parallel rows merging. Each has a
failing log line, a cause, a fix, and a passing log line. That is the basis for the hardware grades.

What the record does not support is "100% Boolean accuracy verified against the physical outputs".
The physical outputs were verified for about a hundred chosen cases, including every wrap and carry
edge the partner specified; the exhaustive check was a software model of the same logic. The article
should keep those two claims separate, because keeping them separate was the partner's own rule.
