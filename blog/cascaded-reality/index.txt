12:T1a6f,<p>In September 2024 I wrote a draft about quantum mechanics, black holes,
and the multiverse. It got two things wrong, and I&#39;m opening with them
because the method matters more than the conclusions: I claimed
information could outrun light (it can&#39;t, the no-communication theorem
closes that door), and I said black holes form when massive stars
collapse (that&#39;s <em>one</em> way, and as of last year it&#39;s provably not the
only one). This is the corrected, tightened, blog-voice version. The full
revision, with all forty-nine references and the claims ledger intact,
lives beside this post, <a href="../the-feedback-equation-thesis/">the thesis itself</a>.</p>
<h2>The cascade</h2>
<p>Here&#39;s the core idea in one breath: the definiteness of everyday reality,
the fact that your coffee cup has <em>a</em> position instead of a smear of
them, is not painted onto the world at the bottom. It accumulates.</p>
<p>Quantum systems in contact with an environment get &quot;measured&quot; constantly:
air molecules, photons, neighboring matter. Only certain states survive
that monitoring (Zurek called the filtering <em>einselection</em>), and the
environment doesn&#39;t just destroy the fragile states, it broadcasts
redundant copies of the survivor. Many observers each intercept a
different fragment and read the same record. That agreement is the entire
content of the word &quot;objective.&quot;</p>
<p>I call the resulting structure <strong>cascaded reality</strong>: outcomes fix, records
propagate, and every record constrains everything downstream. Once the
copies are everywhere, the outcome cannot be unwritten, not because the
underlying law is irreversible (it isn&#39;t), but because you&#39;d have to chase
down every copy. The classical world is the quantum world&#39;s commit log.</p>
<p>(If you&#39;ve pressed the valve on this site&#39;s homepage: that&#39;s the same
mechanics, played, and played honestly. Each press runs a 16-qubit
register through Hadamard gates in the quantum-circuit package and
measures it; the collapse of that register decides which section of the
page comes into existence, or whether the press hits a null field and
nothing does. The null-field odds are not hand-picked either: they derive
from the SHA-256 digest of the first line of the Equation, the same
&quot;ĤΨ=0&quot; that seeds the thesis companion&#39;s hash chain. The constraint sets
the statistics, the measurement collapses them, the record keeps the
outcome. Sections don&#39;t exist until measured; once measured, the record
keeps them.)</p>
<h2>The singularity, inverted</h2>
<p>General relativity defines a singularity by what it costs the theory:
equations stop returning answers. That&#39;s the outside view, singularity
as breakdown.</p>
<p>I read it inverted. At a horizon, entropy saturates the Bekenstein bound:
the maximum information the region can hold, exactly a quarter of the
area in Planck units. Saturation is not failure; it&#39;s a ledger closed.
And time ends there for a specific reason: time is relational bookkeeping
between subsystems (Page and Wootters showed how a timeless whole yields
evolution for embedded clocks, it&#39;s been demonstrated on entangled
photons). When correlations are complete, there are no further
distinguishable states for a clock to count. <strong>The singularity is one
position after one event: the constraint saturated, the cascade complete,
the equation solved.</strong></p>
<p>The deepest equation we have for the whole universe, Wheeler-DeWitt,
has no time in it at all. ĤΨ = 0. Not a law of evolution; a
self-consistency condition. The Big Bang, on this reading, isn&#39;t the
input fed to the equation. It&#39;s what the solution looks like from a clock
embedded inside it.</p>
<h2>The loop, and what 2026 changed</h2>
<p>Chain the pieces: a timeless constraint, a cascade that writes records, a
saturation point where the ledger closes, and then Penrose&#39;s conformal
cyclic cosmology and Smolin&#39;s cosmological natural selection both propose,
in different formalisms, that the closed ledger hands forward the next
initial condition. I call the assembled loop the <strong>Feedback Equation</strong>,
and I label it plainly: speculation.</p>
<p>But its substrate improved on August 12, 2026. Naidu and collaborators
reported MoM-BH*-1 in <em>Nature</em>: an object 660 million years after the Big
Bang, read as a ~100,000-solar-mass black hole growing inside a hydrogen
envelope, a <em>black hole star</em>. Not a stellar corpse: a native, star-like
citizen of the early universe, possibly the swaddled first phase of most
supermassive black holes. A universe that grows black holes as a
first-class process is structurally better built for feedback than the
one in any textbook printed before 2023.</p>
<p>Meanwhile the boundary terms of the whole loop are under live
renegotiation: the local and early-universe measurements of the expansion
rate refuse to overlap at roughly five sigma, and DESI keeps preferring
dark energy that evolves. Wrong decade to declare the equation&#39;s edges
settled.</p>
<h2>No shortcut</h2>
<p>One more claim, the newest in the ledger, <strong>conditional irreducibility</strong>:
wherever an outcome routes through a decoherence-grade record channel, no
computation cheaper than execution exists. You cannot skip to the state at
year 13.8 billion; the running <em>is</em> the solving. The lab-bench version is
a cryptographic hash, preimage resistance is engineered irreducibility,
and I measured the field version myself: in MDTGF, a convergence gate
upstream of SHA-256 could not predict primality downstream of it, ever.
The gate schedules execution. It cannot replace it.</p>
<p>The claim is deliberately conditional, physics is full of compressible
regularities; that&#39;s what laws <em>are</em>, and it&#39;s falsifiable: any
demonstrated shortcut across such a channel breaks it.</p>
<h2>The ledger</h2>
<p>Standing of everything above, in three lines, because a claim doesn&#39;t
count until its label does:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Established:</strong> decoherence, einselection, quantum Darwinism; the
Bekenstein-Hawking area law; Wheeler-DeWitt; Page-Wootters relational
time; the MoM-BH*-1 observation.</li>
<li><strong>Labeled speculation:</strong> conformal cyclic cosmology; cosmological
natural selection (wounded by heavy neutron stars); the black-hole-star
reading of the little red dots as strong inference.</li>
<li><strong>Original here:</strong> cascaded reality; the inverted singularity; the
Feedback Equation as the named loop; conditional irreducibility.</li>
</ul>
<p>The provenance is in the paper, including where the questions came from.
Honesty about that is part of the method. The ledger is the point.</p>
