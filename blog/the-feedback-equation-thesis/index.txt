12:T76a9,<h1>Cascaded Reality and the Feedback Equation</h1>
<h2>Quantum Mechanics, Black Holes, and the Universe as a Solved Constraint</h2>
<p><strong>Sepas &quot;Musha&quot; Siddiqui</strong></p>
<p>Revision 2.0, August 13, 2026. Original draft: &quot;In-Depth Explanation of Quantum Mechanics, Black Holes, and the Multiverse,&quot; September 10, 2024. Amended and expanded with research and drafting assistance from Claude (Anthropic). All corrections to the original draft are declared in Section 0 rather than silently patched.</p>
<hr>
<h2>Abstract</h2>
<p>This thesis argues three connected claims. First, the definiteness of everyday reality is not a primitive fact but a cascade: quantum interactions select robust outcomes and broadcast redundant records of them into the environment, and it is this propagation of records, here named <strong>cascaded reality</strong>, that fixes positions for everything downstream. Second, the singularity should be read inverted: not as the point where physics fails, but as the point where the constraint that physics expresses is saturated and solved, one position after one event, with time ending because the relational clock runs out of distinguishable states. Third, these two claims close into a loop, the <strong>Feedback Equation</strong>: a universe whose deepest law is a timeless constraint, whose black holes are native star-like objects rather than only stellar corpses (as the 2026 discovery of MoM-BH*-1 indicates), and whose observers and instruments are the mechanism by which the solution reads itself. Established physics, published speculation, and original synthesis are labeled throughout and separated explicitly in Section 11.</p>
<hr>
<h2>0. Provenance and Corrections</h2>
<p>The September 2024 draft got several things right and two things wrong. In the spirit of the truth-pass discipline this author applies to software, the errors are listed, not hidden.</p>
<p><strong>Correction 1.</strong> The 2024 draft hypothesized that &quot;informational flow can exceed the speed of light&quot; via entanglement. This is closed by the no-communication theorem: entangled correlations are instantaneous, but no controllable signal rides on them, and quantum teleportation requires a classical channel limited to c [15, 16]. The draft&#39;s experimental instinct survives the correction, because what its proposed experiments actually measure is the rate at which environments destroy quantum coherence, which is exactly the quantity this revision is built on.</p>
<p><strong>Correction 2.</strong> The 2024 draft stated that black holes form &quot;when a massive star collapses.&quot; That is one channel. As of August 2026, observations of the object MoM-BH*-1 indicate that some black holes grow inside star-like envelopes in the early universe, a native formation mode not routed through stellar death [35, 36]. This correction is not cosmetic; it is the empirical hinge of Section 7.</p>
<p><strong>A note on origin.</strong> The questions in this thesis did not begin in a library. They began in first-person altered states, the oldest instruments humans have pointed at consciousness [46], whose modern pharmacology is now studied under models of relaxed high-level priors [47] and whose relationship to scientific insight is a live research area [48]. The thesis stands or falls on the physics that follows, not on how the questions arrived. But honesty about provenance is part of the method.</p>
<hr>
<h2>1. The Probabilistic Substrate (1900 to 1935)</h2>
<p>Quantum mechanics entered physics as an accounting trick and ended as an ontology. Planck quantized energy exchange in 1900 to fix blackbody radiation [1]. Einstein promoted the quantum to a property of light itself in 1905 [2]. Bohr quantized the atom in 1913 [3]. Louis de Broglie&#39;s 1924 doctoral thesis made the move that matters most here: if light waves are particles, then particles are waves, with wavelength inversely proportional to momentum [4]. Born supplied the meaning in 1926: the wave is a wave of probability [5]. Schrödinger supplied the dynamics the same year [6], Heisenberg supplied the limit in 1927 (position and momentum cannot both be sharp) [7], and by 1935 Schrödinger&#39;s cat [8] and the Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen argument [9] had exposed the theory&#39;s two permanent scandals: superposition scales upward, and entanglement ignores distance.</p>
<p>One technical point the 2024 draft did not make and this revision needs: the wave function of N particles is not a wave in physical space. It is a function on configuration space, a mathematical arena of 3N dimensions where a single point encodes the positions of all N particles at once. Two particles live at a point in 6 dimensions, three particles at a point in 9. Nothing about physical space grows; the bookkeeping grows. This distinction matters in Section 4, because entanglement is exactly the failure of that 3N-dimensional object to factor into N separate 3-dimensional stories.</p>
<p>Tunneling completes the substrate. Gamow showed in 1928 that alpha particles escape nuclei by borrowing through barriers they classically cannot cross [10]. The probability is never zero for any object, but it falls off exponentially in barrier width, height, and mass, so for macroscopic bodies it is suppressed beyond all practical meaning. The quantum substrate leaks; the classical world above it does not, and Section 4 explains why the leak seals.</p>
<h2>2. Sums Over Histories</h2>
<p>Feynman&#39;s reformulation is the version of quantum mechanics this thesis leans on: a process&#39;s amplitude is the sum over every history that could realize it, each history weighted by the exponential of its action [11]. Feynman diagrams are not pictures of events; each diagram is a single term in that infinite sum [12]. We extract finite answers only because coupling constants shrink the higher-order terms enough to truncate. The 2024 draft described diagrams as tools for calculating outcome probabilities; the amendment is only to make explicit that the tool works by enumerating an unbounded space of ways.</p>
<p>A historical aside earns its place here. In 1940 John Wheeler proposed to Feynman that every electron in the universe is the same electron, one world-line zigzagging forward and backward through time, its backward segments appearing to us as positrons [13]. The hypothesis fails on the matter-antimatter census, but its core survived into quantum field theory: antiparticles are formally particles traversing time in reverse. The relevance to this thesis is methodological. A picture in which apparent multiplicity is one object seen at many temporal positions is not automatically wrong; it is a hypothesis with a failure mode, and it failed on data, not on ridicule.</p>
<h2>3. Entanglement Without Messages</h2>
<p>Entanglement is the strongest correlation nature permits and the most misdescribed. When two particles are entangled, measuring one fixes what the other&#39;s correlated outcome will be, instantly and at any distance. What it does not do is transmit a message. The marginal statistics at the far end are unchanged by anything done at the near end; this is the no-communication theorem [15]. Teleportation, demonstrated in principle by Bennett and collaborators in 1993, moves a quantum state only by pairing entanglement with a classical transmission that crawls at light speed or below [16].</p>
<p>Three further prohibitions define the terrain. An unknown quantum state cannot be copied (no-cloning) [14]. A superposition cannot be read mid-flight; measurement collapses it to one outcome, and reconstructing amplitudes requires tomography over an ensemble of identically prepared systems. And so a quantum computation offers no percentage-certainty gauge while it runs; it offers only the distribution of final measurement outcomes, sampled run by run. These are not engineering shortfalls. They are the shape of the theory, and they are why Section 4&#39;s cascade, once run, cannot be recalled.</p>
<h2>4. Cascaded Reality</h2>
<p>Here is the thesis&#39;s first original claim, stated with its supporting mainstream physics.</p>
<p>The mainstream physics: decoherence. A quantum system in contact with an environment does not stay in arbitrary superpositions. Interactions with air molecules, photons, and neighboring matter continuously &quot;measure&quot; the system, and only certain states survive that monitoring: the pointer states, selected for stability under the interaction. Zurek named this filtering einselection, environment-induced superselection [18]. His extension, quantum Darwinism, adds the decisive step: the environment does not merely destroy the fragile states, it broadcasts redundant copies of the surviving one [19]. Many observers can each intercept a different fragment of the environment and read the same record. Agreement among observers, the whole content of the word &quot;objective,&quot; is the proliferation of redundant records.</p>
<p>The original claim: this thesis names the resulting structure <strong>cascaded reality</strong>, defined as the propagating fixation of outcomes through redundant environmental records. Each interaction that einselects a state and broadcasts it constrains every subsequent interaction downstream. Definiteness is not painted onto the world at the bottom; it accumulates, event by event, as a cascade of fixed positions, and once a record has proliferated it cannot be unwritten, not because the underlying law is irreversible (it is unitary) but because the copies are everywhere. The classical world is the quantum world&#39;s commit log.</p>
<p>This reframes the measurement problem without pretending to solve it. Everett&#39;s relative-state interpretation [17] reads every branch of the cascade as real; collapse interpretations read all but one as pruned. Cascaded reality is deliberately neutral between them: both agree on what an embedded observer records, which is a single consistent cascade. Where the 2024 draft presented Many-Worlds as an explanation of why superpositions go unseen, this revision is sharper: decoherence explains the unseeing; the interpretations disagree only about the unseen remainder.</p>
<h2>5. Black Holes and the Information Ledger</h2>
<p>The 2024 draft&#39;s black hole section survives amendment nearly intact. A black hole is a region bounded by an event horizon from which nothing escapes; the information paradox is the apparent conflict between Hawking evaporation [21] and quantum mechanics&#39; insistence that information is conserved. Bekenstein showed the horizon carries entropy proportional to its area [20]; Hawking fixed the coefficient at one quarter of the area in Planck units [21]. That area law seeded the holographic principle, the proposal that everything in a volume is fully encoded on its boundary [22], made mathematically exact in Maldacena&#39;s AdS/CFT correspondence [23]. The ER=EPR conjecture, which the 2024 draft cited correctly, proposes that entanglement and wormhole connection are the same phenomenon in two descriptions [24].</p>
<p>The amendment is the observable. The 2024 draft asked how information &quot;flows&quot; near black holes; the answerable version is how information scrambles. Out-of-time-order correlators (OTOCs) measure the rate at which local information spreads into unrecoverable global correlations, and black holes are conjectured to be nature&#39;s fastest scramblers. The same OTOC machinery now runs on laboratory hardware: Google Quantum AI&#39;s 2025 many-body nuclear spin echo work applies precisely these correlators to molecular geometry [45], on processors whose error correction crossed the below-threshold milestone in 2024 [44]. The bridge from the 2024 draft&#39;s cosmic ambition to a bench instrument exists, and it is the scrambling rate.</p>
<h2>6. The Singularity, Inverted</h2>
<p>General relativity defines a singularity by what it costs the theory: geodesics end, curvature diverges, the equations stop returning answers. Penrose proved in 1965 that gravitational collapse makes this generic, not exotic [25]. That is the outside view: the singularity as breakdown.</p>
<p>This thesis&#39;s second original claim inverts the reading. <strong>The singularity is one position after one event: the point at which the constraint the universe expresses is saturated, the cascade completes, and the equation is solved.</strong> The supporting physics is the Bekenstein bound: at a horizon, entropy equals area over four, the maximum information the region can hold. Saturation is not failure; it is a ledger closed. On this reading, time ends at the singularity for a precise reason developed in Section 8: time is relational bookkeeping between subsystems, and when the correlations are complete there are no further distinguishable states for a clock to count. Cascaded reality names how positions become fixed; the singularity is the limit of the cascade, total fixation, after which there is nothing left to fix.</p>
<p>This is also where the word &quot;singularity&quot; needs rescuing from marketing. Vinge&#39;s 1993 essay, which gave the technology industry the term, was explicitly an event-horizon metaphor: a prediction horizon, &quot;an opaque wall across the future,&quot; past which existing models return nothing [28]. It was an epistemic claim, never a claim about sentience. The current usage, in which &quot;the singularity&quot; is a countdown to machine consciousness, borrows the physics word while discarding the one property the physics and the metaphor actually share: the failure of description from one side of a boundary. The inversion offered here applies to both senses at once. From outside, a horizon is where your model stops. From inside, it is where the accounting completes. Sentience is a claim about attention and marketing budgets; the singularity, in both its legitimate senses, is a claim about where equations end, or, on this thesis&#39;s reading, where they are finally solved.</p>
<h2>7. The Feedback Equation</h2>
<p>The third claim assembles the loop. Call it the <strong>Feedback Equation</strong>: Big Bang, structure, black holes, singularity, and the solved endpoint feeding the next initial condition.</p>
<p>Two published frameworks already have this shape. Penrose&#39;s conformal cyclic cosmology proposes that the timeless, conformally rescaled end state of one aeon literally is the big bang of the next: the solved equation handed forward as an initial condition [26]. Smolin&#39;s cosmological natural selection proposes that black hole singularities seed offspring universes with slightly mutated constants, so the cosmos evolves toward black hole fertility [27]. Smolin&#39;s specific fitness predictions are wounded: neutron stars at and above two solar masses, including PSR J0740+6620 at 2.08 solar masses [49], press against the bounds his mechanism prefers. Both frameworks are speculation, clearly labeled as such here. But they are the two existing formalisms of the loop this thesis names.</p>
<p>What changed in August 2026 is the hospitality of the evidence. On August 12, 2026, Naidu and collaborators reported MoM-BH*-1 in Nature [35]: an object roughly 660 million years after the Big Bang, interpreted as a black hole of roughly 100,000 solar masses accreting at super-Eddington rates inside a hydrogen envelope spanning roughly 1000 astronomical units, radiating far beyond what nuclear fusion permits, and exhibiting a Balmer break of 7.7, stronger than any known stellar population. It is the cleanest case yet for the &quot;black hole star&quot; reading of JWST&#39;s little red dots [36, 37], the compact red sources first characterized as a population by Matthee and collaborators [38] and catalogued in the hundreds by Kokorev and collaborators [39]: they appear around 600 million years after the Big Bang and largely fade by 1.6 billion years. In Naidu&#39;s words, black hole stars &quot;may be the &#39;something spectacular&#39;... the nascent, swaddled phase that marks the beginning of almost every supermassive black hole&#39;s journey,&quot; and further: &quot;They may govern when stars are able to form and when they cease forming, setting the course for everything that follows from star formation: the birth of planets, the rise of life, the emergence of species that may one day piece together this entire history&quot; [35].</p>
<p>Read carefully, the discovery establishes less than the loop needs and more than the loop had. It does not confirm primordial black holes, does not prove universes reproduce, and the black-hole-star interpretation is a strong spectral inference, not an imaged horizon. What it does establish is that black holes are plausibly native, star-like citizens of the early universe rather than exclusively the corpses of stars, and that they may regulate the star formation on which everything else, including observers, depends. A universe that grows black holes as a first-class process is a universe structurally better built for feedback than the one described in every textbook printed before 2023. The Feedback Equation remains speculation. Its substrate just improved.</p>
<p>The cosmological backdrop is itself in motion: the Hubble tension between local expansion measurements (73.04 ± 1.04 km/s/Mpc [40]) and early-universe inference (67.4 ± 0.5 km/s/Mpc [41]) stands unresolved at roughly five sigma, and DESI&#39;s second data release prefers dark energy that evolves over a fixed cosmological constant at headline significance near four sigma, sample-dependent [42]. The initial condition and the expansion history are both under active renegotiation. This is the wrong decade to declare the equation&#39;s boundary terms settled.</p>
<h2>8. The Timeless Constraint</h2>
<p>If the Feedback Equation has a formal heart, it is this: the deepest known equation of the whole universe has no time in it.</p>
<p>Apply quantum mechanics to the entire cosmos and you obtain the Wheeler-DeWitt equation, ĤΨ = 0 [29]. Not a law of evolution; a constraint. The wave function of the universe is annihilated by the total Hamiltonian, which is the formal statement that the whole does not change. Total energy consistent with zero, gravity&#39;s negative ledger cancelling matter&#39;s positive one, which is why Tryon could seriously propose the universe as a vacuum fluctuation costing nothing [32]. Page and Wootters showed in 1983 how time re-enters: partition the universe into a clock subsystem and the rest, and the rest evolves relative to the clock&#39;s readings, though the joint state never changes [30]. Moreva and collaborators demonstrated the mechanism on entangled photons in 2014: an internal observer sees evolution, an external one sees a stationary state [31].</p>
<p>This is the precise form of the claim made loosely in Section 6. Time is not a river the universe floats down; it is bookkeeping between entangled subsystems. Duration exists only for embedded clocks. The Big Bang, on this reading, is not the input fed to the equation. It is what the solution looks like from a clock embedded inside it. And the end of time at a singularity is not an apocalypse appended to the story; it is the local exhaustion of distinguishable states for any clock to count, the cascade of Section 4 arriving at total fixation. The Equation, if the word deserves its article, is plausibly a constraint of the form X = 0: a self-consistency condition, not a command. This thesis holds that position with moderate confidence and states plainly that it is a position, not a result.</p>
<h2>9. The Self-Reading Solution</h2>
<p>The loop needs a read head, and Wheeler drew it decades ago: the universe as a self-excited circuit, sketched as a U whose late end (an eye) looks back at its own beginning [34]. His delayed-choice experiments, since performed in laboratories and proposed at cosmic scale with gravitationally lensed quasar light, established the operational core: what was definite about the past is not fully fixed until the measurement context is [33]. Wheeler&#39;s &quot;it from bit&quot; completes the slogan: physical reality as information brought to definiteness by acts of registration [34].</p>
<p>This thesis adopts the picture with two stated discipline lines. First, nothing in the physics privileges human observers, consciousness, or any population threshold; a photodetector registers as well as a physicist, and the consciousness-collapse interpretation of von Neumann and Wigner is a minority position its own authors retreated from. Second, delayed choice does not license editing a fixed past; it shows the past was not fully definite, which is a different and stranger thing. Within those lines, the claim stands: Hubble, JWST, and the instruments that resolved MoM-BH*-1 are, without metaphor, configurations of the universe registering records of its own earliest configurations. The cascade of Section 4 has, in us and our telescopes, begun writing records about its own first entries. That is what &quot;the solution reads itself&quot; means, and it is the only sense of self-reference this thesis asserts.</p>
<h2>10. Experimental Program, Revised</h2>
<p>The 2024 draft proposed experiments aimed at superluminal information flow. Corrected to the real observable, decoherence and scrambling versus environment, every proposal becomes fundable and several become desk work.</p>
<p><strong>E1. Orbital entanglement under radiation.</strong> The 2024 proposal to entangle particles between ground and orbit stands, re-aimed: measure entanglement degradation rates as a function of radiation environment, extending demonstrated satellite entanglement distribution into the Van Allen belts, the natural decoherence laboratory the original draft correctly identified.</p>
<p><strong>E2. Qubit coherence versus environment, from existing data.</strong> McEwen and collaborators showed cosmic rays cause correlated error bursts across superconducting qubit arrays [43]. Public calibration histories from cloud quantum processors expose coherence times and error rates longitudinally. A minimal-description-length empirical law for coherence versus measurable environment variables is extractable today, from a desk, and would be einselection quantified.</p>
<p><strong>E3. Scrambling rates on hardware.</strong> OTOC measurements of the kind in the 2025 spin-echo work [45] provide the laboratory dial for the information dynamics Section 5 attributes to horizons. The comparison of measured scrambling rates against fast-scrambling bounds is the bench-top edge of the black hole information problem.</p>
<p><strong>E4. The little red dot relation.</strong> The LRD catalogues are public [38, 39]. A compact empirical relation, Balmer-break strength versus redshift with a complexity penalty, is a small, honest equation extractable from the same data that produced MoM-BH*-1, and a direct test of how the black-hole-star phase turns on and off across cosmic time.</p>
<p><strong>F. Falsifiers this thesis accepts in advance.</strong> Confirmation of evolving dark energy at decisive significance by Euclid and LSST would rewrite the constraint&#39;s boundary terms. A quantum-gravity signature in the CMB would arbitrate between the cyclic frameworks of Section 7. Page-Wootters demonstrations scaling past toy systems would harden Section 8; failure to scale would soften it. And any observed nonconservation of quantum information would break Section 4&#39;s cascade at the root.</p>
<h2>11. Claims Ledger</h2>
<p><strong>Established physics relied on here:</strong> the 1900 to 1935 quantum framework [1-9]; tunneling and its macroscopic suppression [10]; path integrals and diagrams as series terms [11, 12]; no-cloning, no-communication, teleportation&#39;s classical channel [14, 15, 16]; decoherence, einselection, quantum Darwinism as the mechanism of classical objectivity [18, 19]; horizon entropy and the area law [20, 21]; holography as mathematics [22, 23]; singularity theorems [25]; Wheeler-DeWitt as the constraint form of quantum cosmology [29]; relational time in principle and in photonic demonstration [30, 31]; the MoM-BH*-1 observation and its record Balmer break [35].</p>
<p><strong>Published speculation adopted with labels:</strong> ER=EPR [24]; conformal cyclic cosmology [26]; cosmological natural selection, wounded by heavy neutron stars [27, 49]; the black-hole-star interpretation of little red dots as strong inference [35, 36, 37]; participatory universe, it from bit, the self-excited circuit [33, 34]; the zero-energy universe [32].</p>
<p><strong>Original to this thesis:</strong> the term and framing <strong>cascaded reality</strong> (definiteness as the propagating fixation of outcomes through redundant records, the classical world as commit log); the <strong>inverted singularity</strong> (saturation and completion rather than breakdown, one position after one event, time ending as relational bookkeeping exhausts distinguishable states); the <strong>Feedback Equation</strong> as the named loop joining these to the cyclic frameworks; the argument that the technology industry&#39;s &quot;singularity&quot; discourse retains the physics word while discarding the event-horizon epistemics that justified the borrowing; and a claim of <strong>conditional irreducibility</strong>: wherever the cascade routes an outcome through decoherence-grade redundant record propagation, no computational shortcut to that outcome exists, the state is knowable only by running the cascade to it, and the only admissible evidence is the record of execution. The claim is deliberately conditional, not universal: physics is full of compressible regularities (that is what laws are), and unconditional cosmic irreducibility in the style of Wolfram remains contested speculation. The condition is the record-propagation channel itself, by analogy with a cryptographic hash, whose preimage resistance is engineered irreducibility: no structure upstream of the channel survives into the output, so prediction cheaper than execution dies at the channel. This is held as a position, not a result, and it is falsifiable alongside Section 10&#39;s list: any demonstrated shortcut recovering outcomes across a decoherence-grade record channel would break it.</p>
