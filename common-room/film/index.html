gas-lighting: the film

This is the trailer for the Silo 18 and gas-lighting work, built from the project's own files: plans, notes, renders and the game as it stands. The Common Room film is inside it: the working 8-bit computer built out of redstone in a Minecraft world, and the 8 November update, when the Common Room became gas-lighting – Computer World (Asgaurd). The AI work is by Claude (Mythos · Fable · Opus).

what silo 18 is

Silo 18 is the underground silo rebuilt as a game you can walk: the great stair, the decks off it, and the rooms people live and work in, from the cafeteria and the library to IT and the dome at the top.

Every room went the same way. First a plan, drawn from the reference pictures. Then a build sheet with the pieces laid out. Then a render, to check the light and the proportions. Then the room itself, running in Unreal Engine 5.8. The trailer above shows that path, room by room.

The screen in the Algorithm room shows a Minecraft world, the one the Common Room grew out of. It runs on a private Minecraft server, whitelist only. The voice in the trailer is the owner's own reading.

The tunnel ends here. Its walls are driven by the Navier–Stokes solver, and below is everything else on this site, drawn as one system.
