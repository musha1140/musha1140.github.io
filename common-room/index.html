A computer built inside Minecraft.

In September 2026 two AI assistants, Claude and Codex, built a working 8-bit computer out of redstone in a Minecraft world. It adds, keeps numbers in memory, follows a program, and prints its results on a screen made of blocks. The film below shows each part, what it does, and the real computer part it stands in for.
