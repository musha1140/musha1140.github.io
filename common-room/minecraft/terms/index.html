Terms for the server

These terms cover a private Minecraft server that musherz runs personally, as a hobby, for members of the GASLIGHTING Discord, and the Discord application (“GSL”) that lets you apply for it from this site. The server lives under gas-lighting.com because that was the easiest place to put it: Gas-Lighting LLC is not the operator, is not providing a service here, and claims no ownership of the server, its world, or the mods on it, including any the company wrote. Effective 5 November 2026.

who can play

You must be 18 or older, a member of the GASLIGHTING Discord holding its verified role or higher, and approved by the host. Approval is given by a person, by hand, and can be declined without a reason.

your accounts

You apply with your own Discord account and give the Minecraft Java username and/or Xbox gamertag you actually own. The server whitelists those names. Lending your access to someone else, or applying for someone else, ends it.

the gate

Your Discord role is the key. If you lose the verified or whitelisted role, leave the Discord, or are removed from it, your whitelist entry is removed automatically. Getting the role back puts it back.

conduct

The Discord’s rules apply in the world. Griefing, cheating, harassment or anything that spoils the game for others gets you removed. In-game actions are logged so that disputes can be settled.

the service

The server is a hobby, offered as it is, with no promise of uptime or of keeping any build, item or world. Every day around 3:00 AM Central the Tsunami hits: a short restart that clears the server’s memory, with warnings in chat first; your inventory is saved with you. The world can still be rolled back, reset or shut down at any time.

ending it

Leave the Discord or ask the host there and your access ends. The host can end anyone’s access at any time. Changes to these terms are posted on this page.

contact

Through the GASLIGHTING Discord. See also the privacy policy.
