How to play the deck

Pick a song from the dropdown and press play. The grid under the transport is a drum sequencer: ten instruments, sixteen steps per loop. Tap any cell to place a hit, tap again to remove it, and the loop plays your pattern against the song. The tempo slider bends the whole transport, pitch shifts the melody in semitones, and the synth selector changes the voice. Your computer keyboard plays the drum row directly, a mouse or a touchscreen taps the cells, and if a MIDI controller is plugged in a quiet option appears to hand it the deck. Pads land on the drum slots and keys play the melody voice.

what this deck is not

The deck grew out of an early build: song to binary to a running sum to the prime counting function, heading toward shifted tables of 64 bytes per cell per salt. That path had no stop condition and no defensive evidence trail, so it was stopped, and it is not here.

What is left is an instrument. Nothing on this page counts primes, searches a key space or runs without an end: every loop is bound to the transport, and stop ends it. The only traces of the old build are a fixed, read-only 64-byte XOR stream that colours the melody and places six echo taps, and a sealed export that uses standard PBKDF2-SHA256 (250,000 rounds) with AES-GCM.
