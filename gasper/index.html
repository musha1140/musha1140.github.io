Personal VSL · Value of Statistical Life Profiler

What is your life worth — to you?

Federal agencies, insurance companies, and courts assign a dollar value to a statistical human life every day. The Department of Transportation uses $13.7M. Health and Human Services uses $13.1M. Under 2026 rulemaking, the EPA leaves avoided mortality unmonetized — an omitted cell in the ledger, not a zero. In 1973, Ford valued a burn death at $200,725.

This tool infers your personal VSL from a short set of willingness-to-pay and willingness-to-accept tradeoff questions, then shows you where you land against the benchmarks the rest of the world uses to decide what your life is worth.

VSL = ΔW / Δp
