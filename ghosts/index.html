Ghosts of War Crimes

Since 7 October 2023, people in Gaza have filmed what happened to them, their families, and their streets, and posted it. Archive Genocide has gathered 35,258 of those clips and placed each one on the map with the source behind every placement (dataset version 2026-10-06). In September 2025 the UN Independent International Commission of Inquiry concluded that Israel has committed genocide in the Gaza Strip; Israel rejected the finding.

Spatial footage alone records where harm fell while leaving an institutional “ghost”: who authorized force, how weak threat claims piggybacked on real predicates, and how statutory gates (22 U.S.C. 2378d and 10 U.S.C. 362 Leahy Act unit vetting) collide with continued state anti-BDS pension mandates (40 ILCS 5/1-110.16, challenged via IL HB 2723 & SB 2462 and U.S. Const. amend. I). This workspace unifies the spatial archive, the 2002 Iraq AUMF observable rhetoric engine, statutory and constitutional accountability gates.

Read this first · evidentiary rules
  • • Every Gaza count here measures clips of footage. None of them counts people, deaths, or strikes.
  • • 88% (31,046 clips) are placed only from the place their own caption names and have not been reviewed. 4,212 were placed or checked by an independent geolocator, cited reporting, or an archive reviewer.
  • • At precision levels 1 to 3 a ring marks the centre of a named town, camp, or building, not where the camera stood. Only 4,012 clips (11%) are geolocated to about 50 metres across 932 points.
  • • 71% carry the date they were posted, not the date of what they show. 142 clips have no date and stay counted as undated, never forced into a month.

Illinois Constitution · Article I (Bill of Rights) × HB 2723 & SB 2462

State constitutional baseline governing speech, truth as defense, contract integrity, and remedy

Ill. Const. Art. I text
Section 1 · Inherent and Inalienable RightsIll. Const.
All men are by nature free and independent and have certain inherent and inalienable rights among which are life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness. To secure these rights and the protection of property, governments are instituted among men, deriving their just powers from the consent of the governed.

Just powers require consent of the governed; public pension and procurement statutes cannot compel involuntary material complicity against inherent rights to life and liberty.

Section 2 · Due Process and Equal ProtectionIll. Const.
No person shall be deprived of life, liberty or property without due process of law nor be denied the equal protection of the laws.

Viewpoint-discriminatory state blacklists under 40 ILCS 5/1-110.16 burden property and commercial standing without neutral due process.

Section 3 · Religious FreedomIll. Const.
The free exercise and enjoyment of religious profession and worship, without discrimination, shall forever be guaranteed, and no person shall be denied any civil or political right, privilege or capacity, on account of his religious opinions; but the liberty of conscience hereby secured shall not be construed to dispense with oaths or affirmations, excuse acts of licentiousness, or justify practices inconsistent with the peace or safety of the State. No person shall be required to attend or support any ministry or place of worship against his consent, nor shall any preference be given by law to any religious denomination or mode of worship.

Guarantees liberty of conscience and bars denying civil or political capacity on grounds of conscience.

Section 4 · Freedom of SpeechIll. Const.
All persons may speak, write and publish freely, being responsible for the abuse of that liberty. In trials for libel, both civil and criminal, the truth, when published with good motives and for justifiable ends, shall be a sufficient defense.

Establishes affirmative constitutional protection for publishing documented truth with primary evidence, reinforcing U.S. Const. amend. I and HB 2723 / SB 2462.

Section 8.1 · Crime Victims' RightsIll. Const.
(a)(1) The right to be treated with fairness and respect for their dignity and privacy and to be free from harassment, intimidation, and abuse throughout the criminal justice process. (11) The right to have present at all court proceedings, subject to the rules of evidence, an advocate and other support person of the victim's choice. (12) The right to restitution. (b) The victim has standing to assert the rights enumerated in subsection (a) in any court exercising jurisdiction over the case. The court shall promptly rule on a victim's request. The victim does not have party status. The accused does not have standing to assert the rights of a victim.

Affirms victim dignity, freedom from intimidation, restitution, and independent standing—principles mirrored internationally in Leahy Act (22 U.S.C. § 2378d) remediation duties.

Section 11 · Limitation of Penalties After ConvictionIll. Const.
All penalties shall be determined both according to the seriousness of the offense and with the objective of restoring the offender to useful citizenship. No conviction shall work corruption of blood or forfeiture of estate. No person shall be transported out of the State for an offense committed within the State.

Prohibits forfeiture of estate and out-of-jurisdiction transportation/rendition.

Section 12 · Right to Remedy and JusticeIll. Const.
Every person shall find a certain remedy in the laws for all injuries and wrongs which he receives to his person, privacy, property or reputation. He shall obtain justice by law, freely, completely, and promptly.

Bars statutory schemes that foreclose legal remedy or shield documented injuries from accountability.

Section 16 · Ex Post Facto Laws and Impairing ContractsIll. Const.
No ex post facto law, or law impairing the obligation of contracts or making an irrevocable grant of special privileges or immunities, shall be passed.

Continuing anti-BDS mandates under 40 ILCS 5/1-110.16 impair fiduciary and commercial contract obligations while granting de facto statutory immunity from human-rights divestment.
