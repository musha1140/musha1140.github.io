whitepapers

  • The Song is the Key

    A family of symmetric ciphers whose key material is a musical performance. The same file that plays the room derives the keystream that conceals the message, and the encryption is painted, event by event, in playback time. This paper describes the architecture that survived five generations of the idea and the verification habits that grew around it.

  • MDTGF & the GSL Machine

    Two planes, kept evidentially separate on purpose: a multi-dimensional lattice experiment that measured its own central claim and published the null result, and a from-scratch 32-bit x86 operating system whose every byte is emitted without a compiler. This paper describes both planes, the integrity machinery they share, and the phase now in assembly: the machine leaving the emulator for an always-on node.

  • Zero-Width Integrity

    Invisible Unicode as a carrier: messages and watermarks interleaved into ordinary text using characters that render as nothing, survive copy-paste, and can be found and verified later. This paper describes the carrier, the difference between hiding and watermarking, and the verification pass that turns an invisible mark into a tamper check.

  • Morse Beacon

    Any text, flashed as Morse light from whatever screen is at hand, installable, fully offline, dependent on nothing after first load. This paper describes the timing law, the offline posture, and why the simplest project in the constellation is also the most self-sufficient.

  • QGo, a Zero-Player Universe

    A zero-player Go simulator with an unusual discipline: a hard, tiny, lifetime budget of frontier-model calls, after which every move is played by a locally trained policy. Each finished game becomes a block in a hash-chained ledger and a unit of open training data. This paper describes the budget, the local policy, and the ledger.

  • Forced-Check Search

    A search engine for adversarial decisions, developed against chess because chess grades its own homework: a claimed forcing line either mates or it does not. This paper describes the search shape, rollout pruning, greedy-with-exploration queue management, and elbow-method tuning, at the level of mechanism. The repository is private by design; the engine is described here, not linked.

  • A Deterministic Flow & Proof Engine

    A small engine that turns a body of verbatim normative text into a deterministic workflow: structured intake, derived obligations, an ordered signing flow, and proof runs that re-execute the whole pipeline and diff the artifacts. This paper describes the flow and the proof discipline only. It names no use case on purpose: the engine stays an engine until it can exist in entirety, and that is scheduled for the last phase.
