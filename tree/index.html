SO DID I PROVE IT?

No. And that's the point. This is the one that broke my brain. I built a four-qubit chain in Colab to see if time can fall out of entanglement, following Barontini's 2026 paper. It runs. The numbers follow the paper's shape. The locked case lands exactly where my sheet said it would. Feels like proof, right? It isn't. I never ran it on real hardware. I never added the clock qubit that would make the time in it literal. And some of the curves I showed were drawn, not measured. So what I actually proved is that it isn't proved yet, and exactly where it stops. Everything on this tree grew out of that same habit: build it, measure it, say where it breaks.
