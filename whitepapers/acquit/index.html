Unknown Is Not Zero

gs-acquit, the lattice in practice · with GASPER, the price of a life

The lattice's cell algebra, put to work at a medical practice's front door. Inbound mail becomes a shape on a 64-cell board before anything learns from it; a population of boards evolves against the future, judged lexicographically so that no number of cleared messages can buy a single wrong release; every run leaves a reproducible receipt. Set beside it is GASPER, a profiler for the one exchange rate governments do keep, the value of a statistical life. The two meet on one law: something that could not be determined must never be recorded as a determinate value.

A shape, not words

The practice's public site tells the story in six chapters: a message reaches the practice over the open network, is handed over securely, becomes a shape, waits for a person, is released or held, and the screening improves from the decisions people make. The third chapter is this project. Inside the practice's boundary, a message's tokens are addressed onto an eight-by-eight board of counts, and the words are destroyed there. What travels onward is a pattern, never the message itself.

The trainer enforces that boundary rather than trusting it. Its door refuses any row that carries a raw field, an email-shaped string, a timestamp finer than a day, or a board of the wrong shape, and it refuses by name. It refuses an HTML error page that arrives with a success status, too, a failure that actually happened in continuous integration and is now pinned by a test. Custody is honored by construction: what crosses cannot say who is who or what is what, because it physically no longer can.

One face streamed, five assumed

The board the message brings is one face of a cube. The other five are assumed: latent planes the trainer derives from the stream's own past, the board lately, where this message meets that wake, what recent spam has lit, what recent patients have lit, and a few structural scalars. They are never shipped and never visible in the single observation. Causality is law: a row's cube is sealed before its own label updates any plane, so the future never leaks into the moment and a message's own truth never grades its own features.

Assumed faces may inform a score; they can never substitute for evidence. Evidence is counted only on the visible face, and a message too thin to carry it is never acquitted, however confident the model is.

Tetris, not the cube

There is no solved state. A population of boards evolves on the past and is judged once, at the end, on the chronologically later holdout. The stream moves, and so does the judgment. Selection is lexicographic: a board that releases one spam message loses to every board that releases none, regardless of how many patients the first one cleared. There is deliberately no exchange rate between the two errors, because a fixed rate is a target and a target gets gamed, the lesson of the Perverse Instantiation essay.

Safety floors are eligibility constraints, never objectives. In production the trainer is a co-signer, not a decider: the practice's own gate must already be armed, the sender must authenticate, the stored champion must agree, and a single auto-release later shown to be spam disarms the path. Shipping new weights is a human act; the weekly loop ends at a receipt.

Every relay on disk

A run is a pure function of corpus, seed and configuration. Each run appends one receipt to a ledger: the corpus hash, the seed, the configuration, validation and holdout results, and a hold-everything baseline on the same holdout, so the line is a comparison rather than a claim. The weekly seed derives from the corpus hash, so new data means a new search and the same data re-run means the identical receipt. An always-on runner and the Raspberry Pi can both fire; whichever writes first wins, and the other finds the receipt and bows out.

The trainer also audits its own lineage. At the start of every working session it replays the lattice's song solve through its domain-free core and checks the canceled cells and the nonce against the recorded artifact. If the law has drifted, nothing downstream is trusted until it is explained.

What the ledger says

On the synthetic corpora, which are harness fuel with an invented vocabulary, the champion clears most legitimate messages on the future with zero wrong releases. That proves the machinery, not the mail.

On real post-gate mail the record is plainer, and it is published as such. Replayed from their receipts, the real runs reproduce exactly. Re-scored against the practice's final human labels, every message any champion has ever acquitted on real mail was spam, and no real patient message has yet been acquitted. In the second real week the cube variant released a dozen on the future while the flat variant released none; the following week the practice relabeled a large batch of messages from legitimate to spam, the stream's legitimate share collapsed, and since then every model has held everything. The arming conditions of the practice's own gate are not met on that corpus, so by design no acquittal reaches a person.

That is the honest status of the acquit: the door, the determinism, the causality and the receipts are proven, and acquittal itself is not yet earned. The design is built so that this is a visible, receipted fact rather than a quiet zero.

GASPER, the rate that is kept

The acquit refuses an exchange rate. Government does not: the value of a statistical life is precisely a rate between money and mortality risk, computed as willingness to pay divided by the change in risk. GASPER, the Personal VSL Profiler and companion to the essay on the valuation of human life, asks a short series of willingness-to-pay and willingness-to-accept questions, shows the implied rate after every answer, combines them robustly, calibrates them against revealed behavior, and places the result against the benchmarks agencies actually use.

The essay's sharpest case is the January 2026 decision to leave the health benefits of cleaner air unmonetized, which enters a cost-benefit table as effectively zero. That is the same collapse the lattice and the acquit trainer exist to refuse: an uncertain, probably large quantity recorded as a determinate zero. A wide interval is more accurate than a confident nothing.

GASPER is held to its own thesis here. Its first draft encoded that benchmark as a numeric zero and returned zero for an undefined ratio; both are being corrected to an explicit unmonetized state, so the profiler stops repeating the error it was built to show.

One law, three projects

The lattice keeps three cell states, not two: untouched, on, and canceled, the red zero that looks empty and is not, and its receipt is computed from exactly what disappeared. The acquit trainer keeps a third verdict, hold, for everything it cannot establish. GASPER keeps unmonetized distinct from zero. A crash is noisy; a plausible zero is polite. Each of these systems is built so that the polite zero cannot pass.

Deliberate omissions, this paper describes structure and mechanism, not implementation absolutes. Exact constants, derivation orders, offsets, thresholds, schedules and parameter choices are omitted by design: enough to see the shape and the work, never enough to reconstruct the keys. The repositories and live artifacts remain the reference implementations.
