12:T4ba,<p>An electron doesn&#39;t climb to the next level by practicing. It takes exactly the right amount of energy or nothing happens. The primes feel like that to me.</p>
<p>Riemann&#39;s <a href="/wiki/explicit-formula/" class="wiki-term" title="Riemann's formula writing the prime count as a smooth main term corrected by a sum over the zeta zeros, like a sound written as a sum of frequencies.">explicit formula</a> writes the count of primes as a smooth curve plus a sum over the zeros of the <a href="/wiki/riemann-zeta-function/" class="wiki-term" title="ζ(s) = 1 + 1/2^s + 1/3^s + ..., which Euler showed is a product over the primes; where it equals zero controls how the primes are spaced.">zeta function</a>, the way a sound is a sum of frequencies, and those zeros are spaced the way the <a href="/wiki/energy-level/" class="wiki-term" title="The particular energies a bound quantum system is allowed to have; it jumps between them by absorbing or giving off exact amounts.">energy levels</a> of a chaotic quantum system are spaced. That&#39;s why I say energy level and not apprenticeship. It&#39;s also why I say realization and not solution: the answer isn&#39;t assembled, it&#39;s arrived at.</p>
